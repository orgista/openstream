# OpenStream reference apps — 9 September 2026

The owner selected **Atlas Content Hub** and **Arth Media Center** as inspiration for OpenStream. Use these references when proposing the next UX/design changes. The owner has not yet identified specific screens or interactions to adopt.

## Atlas Content Hub

Primary source checked on 9 September 2026: [Atlas TestFlight invitation](https://testflight.apple.com/join/8CfmY93m).

Its published beta description covers browsing and search, saved titles, add-on manifest setup, managed catalogs, customizable home layout, iCloud sync, and handing supported playback URLs to Infuse on iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV. These are published feature descriptions, not independently tested behavior or visual findings. No Atlas in-app screens were accessed in this pass.

Comparison areas for OpenStream:

1. **Home organization:** inspect how users choose, hide, and arrange catalog rows, and how those choices are presented in Settings.
2. **Discovery to playback:** inspect the path from search or a poster to details, source availability, Play/Resume, episode selection, and returning from playback.
3. **Personal library:** inspect saved-title organization, watch progress, and how sync state is explained.
4. **Source setup:** inspect the number of steps to add a source, validation messages, and recovery from an unavailable source.
5. **Across devices:** compare phone density, iPad navigation, and Apple TV focus behavior against OpenStream’s existing requirements.

These are comparison questions, not claims about Atlas’s execution or commitments to add every listed feature to OpenStream.
